# Contribution and AI collaboration

MADOX is conducted through an AI-native research and engineering workflow. James Allen directs the program; AI agents are substantial technical collaborators. This statement was prepared with AI assistance and describes roles rather than claiming sole authorship of every mechanism.

## James Allen's contribution

James selects problems and research questions, defines system goals and constraints, and sets requirements for persistence, authority and recovery. His role includes establishing invariants and acceptance boundaries, choosing what experiments should answer, evaluating results and deciding when evidence warrants continuation, rejection or redirection.

Two documented examples make that direction concrete:

- A September 21, 2026 architecture clarification establishes the adopted distinction between replaceable cognition and runtime-owned identity, authority and canonical memory.
- An October 15, 2026 file-access decision requires bounded reads within authorized scope, transient content by default and separate permission for retention or expanded access. FD076 develops and tests an operation within those constraints.

These records establish requirements and adopted direction more clearly than the first origin of every underlying idea.

## AI collaborators' contribution

AI agents contribute much of the implementation, including Python code and tests. They also contribute technical designs, local algorithms, debugging, architectural refinements, alternative hypotheses and review. Their role extends beyond translating a finished human design into code.

The FD076 record attributes the race finding to implementation review within this AI-assisted process. It does not establish that James personally found the race, devised the specific correction or wrote its regression. Those mechanism-level contributions are collaborative or uncertain where individual origin cannot be recovered.

## Attribution boundaries

James is responsible for research and system direction, and for decisions about what the work should establish. That does not imply that he independently wrote MADOX or originated every design. Substantial AI implementation and review do not erase his evidenced problem selection, constraints and acceptance decisions.

The selected record supports an account of an iteratively developed system with retained tests, corrections and limitations. It does not independently establish the novelty of each mechanism, sole conceptual ownership, or independent external replication.
